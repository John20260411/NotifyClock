## Uses that need a commercial license

A separate written commercial license is required for, among other things:

- Selling the app, a modified version, or a service based on it.
- Paid distribution, bundling, or preinstallation on devices.
- Publishing a closed-source fork or a proprietary derivative.
- Offering the app or its functionality as a hosted or SaaS product.
- Internal use by a company or other for-profit organization in its
  business operations.
- Using the code in a commercial product, even if the app itself is free.

## Uses that do not need a commercial license

Under the PolyForm Noncommercial terms, these are permitted without a
separate commercial license:

- Personal study, research, experiments, and hobby projects.
- Use by charitable, educational, public research, public safety or
  health, environmental, or government institutions for permitted
  noncommercial purposes.
- Fair use and other rights granted by applicable law.

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, ask before distributing
or deploying.

## How to request a commercial license

Contact the Owner through the public contact methods listed at
<https://github.com/John20260411>. Please include:

- Your name and company or organization.
- The intended product or service.
- Whether you will distribute the app, a modified version, or a hosted
  service.
- Expected distribution scale and revenue model.
- Any requested term, territory, or support arrangement.

Do not send API keys, passwords, or other secrets in the first message.

## Contributions and commercial licensing

The Project requires contributors to accept the
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). This allows the Owner to include
community contributions in both the noncommercial distribution and
commercially licensed versions.
